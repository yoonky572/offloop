Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026. Publisher: Yangxi. This policy covers offloop for Windows.

offloop for Windows stores the choices you enter, their dates and times, and the Focus Days you mark on your Windows device. Historical local data may also contain records from earlier versions of the app.

The current Windows application does not require an account, collect analytics, display advertising, monitor browsing, inspect AI conversations, or upload your records to the publisher. It does not provide cloud backup or cross-device synchronization.

Records remain on your device and are accessible to software and users with sufficient permissions to access your Windows files. The app keeps a previous local copy when saving records. The app does not provide end-to-end encryption or password protection for these files.

Microsoft Store installation, licensing and update services are provided by Microsoft and are subject to Microsoft's own privacy terms. Those services are separate from offloop's local habit records.

Local records are kept until they are removed from your device. Depending on how the app was installed, uninstalling may leave record files or backups behind. If you want to delete your records, close the app and contact the publisher for help locating the applicable files. The publisher cannot recover records that have been deleted from your device.

If you contact the publisher by email, your email address and any information you send are handled through the email service to respond to your request. Do not send private records unless they are needed for troubleshooting.

The public support and privacy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting that website is separate from using the Windows app; GitHub may process technical request information under its own privacy statement: GitHub General Privacy Statement.
