Support

Need help with offloop for Windows? Email yoonky572@outlook.com.

Getting started

When an AI wait ends, choose No scrolling or Scrolled. Open History to review your calendar. Use Mark today when you decide you have earned a Focus Day.

Finding the app

Press Ctrl+Alt+Space or double-click the system tray icon. Closing the window hides it to the tray. To exit, choose Quit offloop from the tray menu.

Your records

Records stay on your Windows device. There is no account, cloud backup or phone-to-PC sync. Please contact support before deleting files or uninstalling if you need help preserving records.

Reporting an issue

Include your Windows version, the app version, and what you were doing. Screenshots are optional; please remove private information first. Do not send passwords or private AI conversations.
